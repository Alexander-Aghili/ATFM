# GPU round 2: complete large root, CPU-tier capacity, repeats: 29 September 2026

This follows [Public H100 workloads](2026-09-29-public-gpu-workloads.md) and
executes stages A, B and D of the
[GPU results report](gpu-results-report/README.md) plan on two new GPU types.
ATFM control is disabled throughout. These runs characterize the ordinary
vLLM/LMCache serving baseline; they do not establish an ATFM policy benefit.

**Outcome:** the 119-request multi-branch root completed for the first time
(H100 SXM, 24 and 48 GiB CPU tier). Doubling the tier removed every L1
allocation warning and lowered median TTFT from 5.6 s to 1.8 s in a single
fixed-order pair. On the A100, three repeats of the short and sequential roots
agreed within about 4%. Compute cost about 5.8 USD.

## Setup

Workloads, model (Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507), AIPerf 0.13.0 replay rules, seed 7
and BFCL checks are unchanged from the
[reproduction guide](../development/gpu-public-workloads.md). New replay options
set the CPU (L1) tier size, LMCache chunk size and per-root/aggregate time
limits; defaults reproduce the first H100 run. `experiments/gpu-cache/pod_setup.sh`
prepares a fresh Pod and `pod_round.sh` runs a plan unattended with a stop
watchdog. Each replay step starts a fresh LMCache and vLLM process pair with an
empty L2 directory; OS page cache, model weights and compiled kernels stay warm
after the first step on a Pod.

## Large root and CPU-tier capacity (H100 SXM)

| CPU tier | Result | Elapsed | TTFT p50 / p95 / max | Latency p50 / p95 | External hit ratio | L1 allocation warnings |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 24 GiB | 119/119, 4/4 children | 1,159 s | 5.60 / 14.74 / 22.22 s | 11.35 / 33.10 s | 86.6% | 15 |
| 48 GiB | 119/119, 4/4 children | 930 s | 1.78 / 7.95 / 31.90 s | 8.17 / 34.24 s | 95.9% | 0 |

Hit ratio is vLLM's external prefix-cache hits over queries for the root.
The 24 GiB run is consistent with the earlier estimate that two ~100k-token
contexts (27.5 GiB of KV at 144 KiB/token) exceed that tier: blocks were
evicted and later recomputed. The 24 GiB elapsed time is just under the old
1,200-second limit, which the 29 September retry exceeded on different hardware.

Limits: one run per setting, in fixed order (24 then 48 GiB), from different
revisions whose only difference is the port wait below. Maximum TTFT rose and
p95 latency did not improve, so the tail is not settled. Round 3 repeats both
settings twice in seeded random order.

## Repeatability and chunk size (A100 SXM)

| Run | Short branch: elapsed, TTFT p50 | Sequential: elapsed, TTFT p50 / p95, latency p50 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rep 1 | 150 s, 0.62 s | 323 s, 1.19 / 3.98 s, 11.02 s |
| Rep 2 | 151 s, 0.60 s | 329 s, 1.21 / 3.99 s, 11.38 s |
| Rep 3 | 152 s, 0.60 s | 328 s, 1.17 / 3.93 s, 11.23 s |
| Chunk 64 | — | 315 s, 1.07 / 3.58 s, 10.60 s |
| Chunk 256 | — | 320 s, 1.07 / 3.53 s, 10.55 s |

All replays completed every request with full output. Chunk 64 and 256 lie
about 10% below the three chunk-16 medians, outside their spread, but each has
one run in fixed order after the repeats. Hit ratios were 87.1% (short) in every repeat and
93.9-94.0% (sequential) across all chunk sizes, so the difference is not more reuse.
The 40-case BFCL matrix gave 39/40 twice; `parallel_9` again returned one call
where two are required, as on the H100 NVL.

## Failed first attempt

The first A100 plan passed BFCL and rep 1, then every later step failed within a
second: the port preflight refused to bind while the previous stack's sockets
were in `TIME_WAIT`. `check_ports` now retries for up to two minutes. The full
plan was rerun; the first attempt is retained under `a100-attempt1/`.
