# GPU round 3: randomized capacity and chunk repeats, Blackwell: 30 September 2026

This repeats the comparisons from [GPU round 2](2026-09-29-gpu-round2.md) with
replicates in seeded random order (`random.Random(20260930)`, plans in
`experiments/gpu-cache/plans/`) and adds a third GPU architecture. ATFM control
is disabled. These runs characterize the vLLM/LMCache serving baseline; they do
not establish an ATFM policy benefit.

**Outcome:** all 17 replay steps (19 complete-root replays) and the 40-case
BFCL matrix completed on H100 SXM,
A100 SXM and RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell. On one H100 SXM Pod, a 48 GiB CPU tier
halved the 119-request root's duration and cut median TTFT 5.7x against 24
GiB, with two runs per setting in the order 48, 24, 24, 48. The A100 showed the
same direction. Chunk sizes 64 and 256 again lowered sequential-root TTFT against
16 on both GPUs tested. Compute cost about 7.3 USD.

## Setup

Workloads, model, replay rules and checks are unchanged from round 2 (revision
`d694c18`). Every replay step starts fresh LMCache and vLLM processes with an
empty L2 directory; page cache, weights and compiled kernels stay warm after a
Pod's first step. Comparisons below are within one Pod.

## CPU-tier capacity on the 119-request root

| Pod, order | CPU tier | Elapsed | TTFT p50 / p95 / max | Latency p50 / p95 | Hit ratio | L1 warnings |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| H100 SXM, 1st | 48 GiB | 591 s | 2.02 / 7.21 / 15.71 s | 5.12 / 15.95 s | 95.9% | 0 |
| H100 SXM, 2nd | 24 GiB | 1,126 s | 10.93 / 19.78 / 33.82 s | 13.77 / 30.78 s | 89.4% | 32 |
| H100 SXM, 3rd | 24 GiB | 1,159 s | 12.06 / 22.78 / 27.73 s | 14.10 / 30.83 s | 89.6% | 18 |
| H100 SXM, 4th | 48 GiB | 588 s | 2.00 / 6.21 / 17.32 s | 5.23 / 17.42 s | 95.9% | 0 |
| A100 SXM, 1st | 24 GiB | 1,208 s | 6.63 / 29.69 / 50.38 s | 12.15 / 53.94 s | 90.4% | 8 |
| A100 SXM, 2nd | 48 GiB | 966 s | 3.16 / 12.81 / 37.49 s | 9.68 / 38.51 s | 95.9% | 0 |

Every run completed 119/119 requests and 4/4 child branches. On the H100 the
48 GiB runs bracket the 24 GiB runs, so warm-up order cannot produce the
difference; within-setting spread is 1-3% in elapsed time against a 1.9x gap.
The mechanism is visible in the counters: at 24 GiB allocation fails, the
external hit ratio falls by about six points and missed prefixes are
recomputed. The A100 has one run per setting; its larger tail values at both
sizes fit slower recomputation.

The round-2 H100 SXM (another data center) took 930 s at 48 GiB against 588-591 s
here with identical hit ratios. Hosts with the same GPU model can differ by
more than the cache effect under study, so capacity or policy comparisons must
be paired within a Pod. The manifests do not yet record host CPU or PCIe
details that could explain the gap.

## Chunk size on the sequential root

| Pod | Chunk 16 | Chunk 64 | Chunk 256 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A100 SXM, TTFT p50 | 1.52, 1.47 s | 1.24, 1.26 s | 1.22, 1.23 s |
| A100 SXM, latency p50 | 11.90, 11.53 s | 10.86, 11.15 s | 11.08, 10.84 s |
| RTX PRO 6000, TTFT p50 | 0.68, 0.69, 0.68 s | 0.62 s | 0.62 s |
| RTX PRO 6000, latency p50 | 6.56, 6.67, 6.63 s | 6.21 s | 6.20 s |

A100 order was 16, 64, 16, 64, 256, 256; the Blackwell chunk runs followed two
chunk-16 repeats. Chunks of 64 or 256 tokens lowered median TTFT by about 17%
on the A100 and 9% on the Blackwell GPU, with no overlap between settings. The
effect on full latency is smaller (3-7%) because decode dominates it. Hit
ratios were 93.9-94.0% at every size, so the gain is not more reuse; fewer,
larger transfers are a plausible cause that this run does not isolate. 64 and
256 are indistinguishable here. The A100 chunk-16 medians are higher than on
the round-2 A100 Pod (1.17-1.21 s), another host difference.

## Blackwell compatibility and repeats

The locked CUDA 13.0 environment installed and served on the RTX PRO 6000
without changes. BFCL passed 39/40 with the same `parallel_9` single-call
result seen on H100 NVL and A100. Two short/sequential repeats agreed within
2%: short 95-97 s (TTFT p50 0.35 s), sequential 215-216 s (TTFT p50
0.68-0.69 s), with hit ratios identical to the A100's.
